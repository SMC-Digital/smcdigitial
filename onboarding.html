03

What we need from you

Gather these and send them through. Do not worry if you cannot fill every gap, we will chase the rest.

Your business

  • Business name and ABN
  • Best phone and email for enquiries
  • Address or service area, the suburbs you cover
  • Opening hours
  • Licence or registration numbers, if you have them
  • How long you have been trading

Your services

  • List of your services
  • The main job you want more of
  • Who your ideal customer is
  • What makes you better than the other options nearby

Brand and photos

  • Your logo, best quality file you have
  • Brand colours, if you know them
  • Photos of your work, team or vehicles, a shared folder is perfect
  • A few reviews or testimonials
  • Social profiles and any current website

Website content

  • Pages you want, we suggest Home, Services, About, Gallery and Contact
  • The one message a visitor should get
  • Any wording you already have
  • What you want people to do: call, quote or book

Your domain

  • Do you already have a domain, such as yourbusiness.com.au
  • Where it is registered, such as GoDaddy or Crazy Domains
  • If you need one, we help you choose and buy it
  • You will share access when we are ready to connect it

Accounts you own

  • Your Google account for Ads, Analytics and Business Profile
  • Your Facebook page
  • A card for your ad spend, added to your own accounts
  • You approve our access requests. Every account stays in your name

Ads and leads

  • Your monthly ad budget, separate from the build and paid to Google and Meta directly
  • The jobs and areas you most want to win
  • Any competitors worth knowing about
  • Where leads should land: email, phone or text, and who follows up

Anything else

  • Any deadlines or busy seasons we should plan around
  • Anything you love or hate on other websites
  • Questions for us, ask away

You own everything, always

Your website, domain, Google Ads, Meta, analytics and Business Profile accounts are all in your name. We build and manage inside them with your permission. If you ever move on, you keep every account, all the data and the full history. No hostage accounts.
