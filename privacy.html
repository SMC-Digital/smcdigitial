Privacy Policy

Last updated 21 September 2026

SMC Digital (we, us, our) respects your privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use, store and protect your personal information, in line with the Australian Privacy Act 1988 and the Australian Privacy Principles.

What we collect

When you contact us, enquire through one of our forms or ads, or become a client, we may collect:

  • Your name, email address and phone number
  • Your business name, trade or service, location and service area
  • Information you choose to share about your goals, budget and marketing
  • Technical data such as your device, browser and how you use our website, collected through analytics and advertising tools

How we use it

  • To respond to your enquiry and provide our services
  • To build, run and improve your website and advertising campaigns
  • To contact you about your project and, where you have agreed, to send relevant updates
  • To measure and improve the performance of our own marketing

Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only where needed to deliver our services, including with trusted providers such as Google, Meta, hosting, analytics, email and form services. These providers process data on our behalf and are bound to keep it secure. Some may store data outside Australia.

Cookies and tracking

Our website and landing pages use cookies and similar tools, including Google Analytics, Google Ads and the Meta Pixel, to understand traffic and measure ad performance. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

How we protect it

We take reasonable steps to keep your information secure and to hold it only for as long as we need it for the purposes above or as required by law.

Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, or ask a question about how we handle it, at any time. Just email us and we will help.
