Before you ask.

Is it really free?

Yes. No invoice, no obligation. If you take the document and run the offers yourself, that's a fine outcome.

We're member-owned with a committee. Does that matter?

No, most clubs we work with are. The audit is written to be forwarded and read by a board, so you're not the one having to explain it.

What budget does a club need?

Most single-course clubs start between one and three thousand dollars a month in ad spend. We work backwards from your green fee and membership values, and if the maths doesn't support it we'll say so.

No. Month to month, thirty days notice.
